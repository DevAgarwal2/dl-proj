OpenMed

An AI that reads chest X-rays and explains its answer with real medical references — fully offline.

1 The problem

What an AI normally gives you
Cardiomegaly: 89%
  • ❓ Why? — no reason
  • ❓ Where? — didn't show the image region
  • ❓ So what? — doesn't explain the finding
  • ❓ Evidence? — no sources to check

A dead number you cannot trust or act on.

What OpenMed gives you
Cardiomegaly (89%) — model looked at the heart region (heat-map). "The chest X-ray shows an enlarged cardiac silhouette, a key sign of cardiomegaly [1]. Common causes include hypertension, cardiomyopathy and valvular disease [2]. This is a research tool, not medical advice." [1] Identifying cardiomegaly in chest X-rays… [2] Cardiomegaly on chest radiographs as a predictor of heart disease…

A number turned into an explanation with proof.

2 How it works — four steps, all on your laptop

🫁

1. Predict

A fine-tuned CNN (DenseNet121) scores 14 chest conditions from the X-ray.

➜
🔥

2. Show where

Grad-CAM highlights the exact image region behind each prediction.

➜
📚

3. Find evidence

EmbeddingGemma-2 searches ~500 real medical abstracts for relevant passages.

➜
✍️

4. Explain

A local 1.2B model writes a short, cited explanation — and refuses if evidence is weak.

No internet. No cloud. No per-query cost.

4 What it does not do

  • Chest X-rays only — exactly 14 conditions. Not CT, MRI, or other body parts.
  • Not a diagnosis — no clinical validation, not a medical device.
  • Research-grade accuracy — 64×64 images, so it produces false alarms.
OpenMed is a research / learning prototype. Always consult a doctor.
Built with PyTorch · timm · MedMNIST · Grad-CAM · EmbeddingGemma-2 · llama.cpp · LFM (Liquid AI) · FastAPI · Streamlit.
